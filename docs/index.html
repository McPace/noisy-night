Privacy Policy

Noisy Night  ·  Last updated: September 2026

🔒 The Short Version

Noisy Night collects absolutely no personal data. It does not connect to the internet, does not require a login, contains no ads, and does not track anything about you or your device.

📡 Data Collection

We collect zero data. Specifically:

  • No personal information
  • No device identifiers
  • No usage statistics or analytics
  • No crash reports sent to any server
  • No location data
  • No cookies or tracking

The app runs entirely on your device. No data ever leaves it.

🔑 Permissions Explained

The app requests the following Android permissions:

WAKE_LOCK
Keeps the audio engine running while your screen is off so the noise generator plays reliably all night.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK
Allows audio to continue playing in the background as a media service, which also shows the persistent notification controls.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Required on Android 13+ to display the playback notification that lets you stop or control the noise generator from the notification shade.

None of these permissions are used to collect any data.

🌐 Internet Access

Noisy Night does not use the INTERNET permission and makes zero network requests. All audio is generated procedurally on your device.

👶 Children's Privacy

Because we collect no data whatsoever, Noisy Night is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL. Given the nature of the app (no data collection, no network), significant changes are highly unlikely.
